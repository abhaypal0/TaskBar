# Privacy Policy for Glass Task Manager

**Effective Date:** January 31, 2026

## 1. Overview
The "Glass Task Manager" extension is built as an open-source productivity tool. This Privacy Policy informs users that we do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information.

## 2. Data Collection and Usage
- **Personal Data:** This extension does NOT collect any personal information (such as names, email addresses, or browsing history).
- **Task Data:** All tasks created within the extension are stored locally on the user's device using the `chrome.storage.local` API. 
- **Data Transmission:** No data is transmitted to any external servers, third parties, or the developer. Your data stays on your machine.

## 3. Data Retention
Your tasks are saved on your local browser profile. If you uninstall the extension or clear your browser data, your tasks will be permanently deleted.

## 4. Permissions Justification
- `storage`: Required solely to save your task list locally so that your data is not lost when you close the browser.

## 5. Third-Party Services
The "Buy Me a Coffee" link is an external service. If you click it, you will be directed to their website, which has its own privacy policy.
